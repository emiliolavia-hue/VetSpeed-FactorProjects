    Pergo hasn’t delivered

    • A big investment, live for about two and a half years
    • Got your drivers mobile for the first time
    • Promises since, some critical to legislation tracking, not delivered
    • Confidence in the supplier is falling fast
    hourglass_bottom Medisort today

    A clever system that can’t stay

    • Delphi, not upgraded in 14 years
    • Built by a one-man operation, now in his early 70s
    • He knows the No-Loss link best, so it should be specified while he’s available
    • Pergo there is unlikely, so its replacement will most likely run Vetspeed too
    gavel Regulation

    Digital Waste Tracking is live

    • Since 1 October 2026, permitted receivers in England and Wales report every load within two working days
    • Phase 2 for carriers expected in 2027: beta in April, enforcement from October
    • Your next system has to keep up as the rules arrive

    • Around £7m of consumables held and sold on your rounds
    • Pergo has no concept of stock or sales orders
    • So it runs on “negative collections” and bespoke reports
